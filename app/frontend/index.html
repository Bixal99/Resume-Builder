Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Is ResumeBuilder really 100% free?

Yes, completely. There are no paywalls, no trial expirations, no subscriptions, and no hidden watermarks stamped on your downloaded PDF.

Will my PDF pass Applicant Tracking Systems (ATS)?

Yes. All 8 templates use clean single-column or ATS-approved layouts with standard headings, semantic hierarchy, and selectable vector fonts that parse cleanly.

How does the Live ATS Keyword Matcher work?

Paste the text of any job posting you are targeting. Our engine tokenizes the keywords, cross-references them against your resume content, and calculates your match percentage.

Is my resume data kept private?

Yes. Your data is stored locally in your browser's private local storage. We do not sell, track, or commercialize your personal information.
